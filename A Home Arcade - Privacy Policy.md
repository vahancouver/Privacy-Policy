# Privacy Policy — A Home Arcade

_Last updated: ‹2026-11-19›_

A Home Arcade does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
- **No tracking.** The app does not track you and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **No network transmission.** Your game progress (scores, streaks, puzzle state) is saved only on your
  device, in the app’s private App Group container, so the app and its widgets can share it. It never
  leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our servers. Removing
the app removes its locally stored game state.
